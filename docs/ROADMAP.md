# EduCare Management System

# Product Roadmap

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Living Document  
**Last Updated:** July 2026

---

# 1. Purpose

The EduCare Product Roadmap outlines the planned evolution of the platform from its initial Minimum Viable Product (MVP) through future releases. It serves as a strategic guide for development, ensuring that new features align with the product vision, customer needs, and YapiTech's engineering principles.

The roadmap is iterative and will evolve as customer feedback, technological advancements, and business priorities change.

---

# 2. Product Vision

EduCare aims to become a modern, cloud-based School Management Platform that simplifies administration, improves collaboration, and enables educational institutions to make better decisions through reliable data and intelligent automation.

---

# 3. Development Strategy

Development follows an incremental delivery model.

Each release should:

- Deliver measurable business value.
- Be fully tested.
- Maintain backwards compatibility where practical.
- Build upon a stable foundation.
- Follow YapiTech Engineering Standards.

EduCare is the flagship SaaS product of YapiTech Innovations.

- EduCare Management System
